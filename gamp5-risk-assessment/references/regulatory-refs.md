# Regulatory References for Risk Assessment Documents

Include the relevant references in Section 1 (Purpose and Scope) based on the applicable
regulatory framework for the system being assessed.

---

## Always Include (Core References)

| Reference | Full Citation | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| GAMP 5 2nd Ed | ISPE GAMP® 5: A Risk-Based Approach to Compliant GxP Computerized Systems, Second Edition. ISPE, 2022. | All assessments |
| ICH Q9 | ICH Q9(R1): Quality Risk Management. International Council for Harmonisation, 2023. | All assessments |

## Standard Regulatory Language Templates

Use these in Section 1 of the document as appropriate:

**ICH Q9 Principles (always include for pharma QRM):**
> "The evaluation of the risk to quality should be based on scientific knowledge and
> ultimately link to the protection of the patient; and the level of effort, formality
> and documentation of the quality risk-management process should be commensurate with
> the level of risk." — ICH Q9(R1)

**EU Annex 11 Principle (for EU GMP systems):**
> "Where a computerised system replaces a manual operation, there should be no resultant
> decrease in product quality, process control or quality assurance. There should be no
> increase in the overall risk of the process." — EU GMP Annex 11

**GAMP 5 Risk Definition:**
> "Risk: The combination of the probability of occurrence of harm and the severity of that
> harm." — GAMP 5 Second Edition, Chapter 5, citing ICH Q9
